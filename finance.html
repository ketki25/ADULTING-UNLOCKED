Common Questions

Contact Us
Is Adulting Unlocked completely free to use?
Yes! All core features are completely free for students. We believe every engineering student deserves access to good resources regardless of their financial background.
Are the hostel listings verified?
We manually verify all hostel listings before they go live. Verified hostels have a blue checkmark. We also rely on community reviews to maintain quality. Always visit before booking.
How does the mentor matching work?
Our algorithm matches you with mentors based on your branch, interests, career goals, and skill gaps. You can also browse manually and send chat requests to any available mentor.
Is the Grad Mind vent wall truly anonymous?
Absolutely. No login required to post. No IP tracking for content. Posts are entirely anonymous by design. We only moderate for harmful content to keep the space safe.
Which cities and colleges does this cover?
We currently have strong coverage in Pune, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, and Hyderabad. We're expanding rapidly. The career, finance, and mental health features work pan-India.
Can I become a mentor?
Yes! If you're a senior or recent graduate, you can apply to become a mentor from the StudyBuddy module. All mentors are verified. It's free and incredibly rewarding.
